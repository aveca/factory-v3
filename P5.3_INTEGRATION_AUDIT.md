## 3. Jitter Synchronization Prevention Measurement

**Key metric**: spread of retry wait times across 50 missions experiencing quota exhaustion

| Condition | Wait Time Distribution | Synchronization |
|---|---|---|
| **Without jitter** | All 50 missions wait exactly `min(2^attempts, 30)s` | **Perfect synchronization** (spread = 0s) — thundering herd on quota renewal |
| **With jitter** (±2s, seed=42) | 50 missions spread across ~4s window | **Prevented** (spread ≈ 3.87s) — some missions succeed earlier |

**Per-attempt results (with jitter, seed=42)**:
- Attempt 1: base=2s, spread=3.87s (range: 0.03s to 3.89s), mean=1.80s
- Attempt 2: base=4s, spread=3.87s (range: 2.03s to 5.89s), mean=3.80s

**Benefit**: Temporal spread prevents concurrent quota consumption. Some missions succeed earlier (consuming quota), others retry when more quota is available, reducing total quota exhaustion stops in multi-worker scenarios.

## 5. time.sleep() Blocking Assessment

**Finding**: The `time.sleep(backoff)` in the `refus_quota` category is acceptable and not "unnecessary" blocking.

**Rationale**:
- **Bounded duration**: `max(0, min(2^attempts, 30) + random.uniform(-2, 2))` → max ~32s (attempt 5+, but limited by `max_retries=3` → max attempt 3 → base=8s + 2s jitter = max ~10s)
- **Mission preserved durably**: The mission stays `queued` in the SQLite queue during the sleep; it is not lost
- **Worker not lost**: The worker tick continues after sleep; the mission can be re-claimed
- **Jitter purpose**: The sleep + jitter is specifically designed to prevent thundering herd when multiple workers experience quota exhaustion simultaneously. Without it, all workers would retry at the exact same moment, wasting quota and causing repeated 429s.
- **Alternative considered**: Removing the sleep would cause immediate synchronized retries (measured: spread = 0s), which is worse for quota conservation.

**Recommendation**: Keep the `time.sleep(backoff)` + jitter. It is a localized, reversible improvement that prevents a worse problem (thundering herd). No change needed.
